Alexander Purcell has spent his career moving between two disciplines that, to him, were never really separate. Through Atelier Purcell, the furniture and lighting collection he launched in 2019, he designs objects—brass paired with eucalyptus, bronze with marble—meant to be lived with for a long time. Through Purcell Design, he shapes narrative-driven interiors and hotels.
