Editors note: This post was originally published on June 3, 2014 and was updated on November 12, 2025. Allan Jenkins’ work combines the best of traditional and modern photography techniques. For example, his Victorian-era cyanotype prints are created from digital negatives and his self-published print books are based on his hand-sketched notebooks.
