Client testimonials are one of the few marketing tools you can’t buy or fake. They’re proof that real clients trusted you, got tangible results, and felt strongly enough about the experience to talk about it. That combination of trust, outcome, and emotion makes client testimonials one of the most powerful assets in your entire business.
